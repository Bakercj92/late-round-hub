How to read this

  • The order is the robust result. Seats rank 1→14 monotonically — no plateau — and the ranking survives every sensitivity variant (no-usage, flat/steep value curve, your real script: spearman +1.00, bar one 11↔12 dead heat inside the noise). Seat 1 is best and seat 14 worst in all of them.
  • The size of the edge is a range, not a number. Seat 1 over seat 14 ≈ +6.2% (37 VOR) drafting the way you actually drafted '24–'25 (the floor) up to +11.7% (72 VOR) drafting best-available perfectly every pick (the ceiling). Reality sits between, likely nearer the floor.
  • Why it slopes: this year's top-heavy talent under a competent drafter — not the per-manager opponent modeling. The manager models reproduce the real 2025 draft within ~5 picks at every position/round, supply the neighbor effects, and confirm the ranking holds against real opponents; they don't create the gradient.
  • Luck range (p25–p75) is draft-luck only — who falls to you across 400 sims. Season/player variance is not modeled, and it dwarfs seat choice.
  • Neighbor effects are second-order and directional (±4–7 VOR ≈ 1%): a manager who shares your lean sitting just ahead hurts; a QB-hoarder ahead helps. Only 2 of 26 measured estimates are strongly resolved (rrishi45 ahead of seat 3, mountaindew11 ahead of seat 9) — use as a tiebreaker, not a driver.
